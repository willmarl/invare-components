General-purpose NPN BJT for switching small loads (relay coils, LEDs, buzzers). Put a base resistor (~1k) between GPIO and base. Add a flyback diode if switching an inductive load.

- Type : NPN (TO-92)
- Collector-Emitter Voltage : 30V – 40V
- Collector Current : ~600mA max (stay well under that for heat)
- Pinout (flat side facing you, left to right) : Emitter, Base, Collector — confirm on your part, clones swap this
