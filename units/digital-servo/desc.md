High-torque metal-gear digital servo (DS3218MG). Needs its own beefy 5–6V supply — stall current is amps, not milliamps. Signal is still standard servo PWM. Some units are sold as 180° or 270°; check pulse range.

- Operating Voltage : 4.8V – 6.8V
- Stall Torque : ~19 kg·cm @ 5V, ~21.5 kg·cm @ 6.8V
- Stall Current : ~1.5A – 2.6A (size the PSU for stall, not idle)
- Idle Current : ~4–5mA
- Speed : 0.16 sec/60° @ 5V
