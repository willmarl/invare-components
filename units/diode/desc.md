1N4007 silicon rectifier diode. Band marks the cathode. Fine for flyback across motors/relays/solenoids and for polarity protection. Not a Schottky — forward drop is ~0.7–1.1V so it wastes a bit of voltage.

- Reverse Voltage : 1000V
- Forward Current : 1A
- Forward Drop : ~0.7V – 1.1V
