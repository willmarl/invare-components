L293D dual H-bridge for two DC motors or one bipolar stepper. Logic and motor supplies are separate. Built-in clamp diodes on the D variant.

- Logic Voltage (Input from microcontroller) : 4.5V – 7V
- Load Voltage (Power that the pin from driver can handle) : 4.5V – 36V
- Current per channel : 600mA continuous, 1.2A peak
- Caveat : ~1.4V drop, so a 6V motor on 6V supply will be sluggish
