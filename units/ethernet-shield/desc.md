W5500 Ethernet shield — wired TCP/IP on Arduino-style boards over SPI. Chip is 3.3V with 5V-tolerant I/O. Pin 10 is chip select for Ethernet, pin 4 for the microSD slot; those pins are spoken for. W5500 and SD share SPI, so deselect the one you are not using.

- Operating Voltage : 5V from the Arduino (chip itself 3.3V)
- Speed : 10/100 Mbps
- Interface : SPI
- Sockets : 8 simultaneous
