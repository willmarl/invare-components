MP3-TF-16P / DFPlayer Mini. Plays MP3/WAV from microSD, controlled over UART (9600 baud) or standalone buttons. RX is 3.3V TTL — put a 1k resistor in series if talking from a 5V MCU. Speaker pins drive up to ~3W; use DAC_L/R for headphones/amp.

- Operating Voltage : 3.2V – 5V (5V recommended for decent speaker volume)
- Interface : UART 9600 baud
- SD : FAT16/FAT32, typically up to 32 GB
- Current : ~20mA idle, up to ~200mA playing into a speaker
