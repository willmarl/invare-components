74HC595 serial-in parallel-out shift register — 3 MCU pins (data, clock, latch) become 8 outputs, chainable. Outputs are not high-current; don't drive motors from them. HC family wants 2–6V; 5V is the usual hobby voltage.

- Operating Voltage : 2V – 6V (3.3V / 5V typical)
- Outputs : 8 (chain more chips for 16, 24, …)
- Current per output : ~35mA max, ~70mA total for the chip
