LM2596 adjustable buck (step-down) converter. Set the output with the trim pot and verify with a multimeter before connecting a load. Input must be a couple volts above the output. Cheap modules are often optimistic about the 3A rating — they get hot.

- Input Voltage : 4V – 40V
- Output Voltage : 1.25V – 37V (adjustable, must be less than input)
- Current : 3A claimed, ~2A more realistic without extra cooling
