NRF24L01 2.4 GHz transceiver over SPI for short-range MCU-to-MCU links. Power is 3.3V only — 5V will kill it. Put a 10µF cap across VCC/GND on the module; cheap clones brown out when transmitting. Datasheet range is optimistic; indoor is more like 10–30 m unless you have the PA+LNA (big antenna) version.

- Operating Voltage : 1.9V – 3.6V (3.3V typical, not 5V tolerant)
- Band : 2.4 GHz
- Interface : SPI
- Range : ~10–30 m indoor (PA+LNA modules much farther)
